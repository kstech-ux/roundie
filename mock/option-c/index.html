01 · FEATURES

What other golf apps can't do.

Most apps track scores. ROUNDIE captures the memory. Every round becomes a one-of-a-kind canvas — saveable, shareable, returnable. Pair it with a 3-pillar score that values how you play AND who you are.

🎨 ROUND CARD · ONLY ON ROUNDIE

18 holes become a one-of-a-kind color grid.

No two grids are alike. Eagle gold, par green, bogey blue — your day, painted. Save it as a Round Card you can hold, share, and revisit years from now. Other apps don't do this.

🪪SCORE

3-pillar ROUNDIE Score.

SKILL · VIBE · STYLE. Climb 7 ranks. Pure earning, no pay-to-win.

🔥KUDOS

Three Kudos for golfers.

Nice Shot · Nice Fight · Best Buddy. Real comments, golf etiquette.

🏆COMPETITIONS

Host a comp in 3 taps.

Auto-scoring, animated result reveal, individual titles auto-shared.

📱FEED

Friends' rounds, in one beautiful timeline.

Round Cards take center stage. Photos when added. Every post tells the day's story.

🔒PRIVACY

You decide what gets shared.

Per-round visibility. Mute · Block · per-photo controls. Supabase Tokyo. Never sold.
